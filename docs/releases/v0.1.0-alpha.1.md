## Remaining alpha limitations

- Pareto/rank selection and adaptive AI policies require separately versioned work.
- Family windows preserve edges and methods; explicit extinction aggregation and
  genetic-distance diversity are not implemented.
- No public voting service, full process-level CPU/memory profile, human task study,
  alternate provider, generated winner
  materialization or broad Oracle/APEX compatibility claim.
- Automatic proxies favor large text and long single-column layouts. Equal candidate
  slots differ from equal unique browser work. Five seeds are descriptive only.

This is an implemented, evidence-bearing **alpha**, not completion of every ambition
in the master research brief. Remaining continuous-operation and research limitations stay explicit.
